AccessMove Privacy Policy

Updated November 6, 2026

AccessMove is a free, not-for-profit iPhone app that finds accessible walking routes. This policy explains what information AccessMove uses, where it goes, and what stays on your iPhone. AccessMove has no accounts and no advertising, doesn't track you, and doesn't sell or share your information.

AccessMove asks what you need (for example, "step-free routes" or "captions instead of voice"), not about any disability or health condition.

Information that stays on your iPhone

These are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your choices in What I need (for example, step-free routes, captions and vibrations instead of voice, noise and crowds bother me).
  • Your choices in Just for now (for example, walk more slowly, rest stops more often).
  • Sensory comfort settings, voice and guidance settings.
  • Saved places (Home, Work, favorites) and recent places.
  • Your trusted contact's name and phone number.
  • Vibration lesson and quiz progress.

Deleting AccessMove from your iPhone deletes this information.

Location

AccessMove uses your location while you use the app, and during a trip (including when your screen is locked), to find routes and guide you. To do this, some location information is sent to these services:

  • openrouteservice (HeiGIT, Germany) receives your starting point, destination, and route needs (such as "avoid steps" or "gentle slopes") to calculate an accessible route. It does not receive your name or your What I need choices.
  • Google Maps shows the map. Google may receive your device's approximate location and network information to load map images.
  • Apple Maps searches for places you type or say, and finds nearby places (for example, What's around me, pharmacies, parks).

Anonymous community route information

To help rank routes, AccessMove sends anonymous counts to Google Firebase. The map is divided into small squares (about 100 meters across). For each square on a route, AccessMove may add:

  • one to the number of times a route passed through it,
  • your route rating (1 to 5 stars), if you choose to rate a trip,
  • an "obstacle reported" note, if a route was blocked.

These counts don't include your name, an account, your What I need choices or a device ID. Like any internet service, Google Firebase may process technical information such as your IP address when the app connects to it.

Microphone and speech

  • Speak your destination and Siri: when your iPhone can recognize speech by itself, your words stay on your iPhone. Otherwise, Apple's speech recognition processes them under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Noise heads-ups: if you choose "Noise and crowds bother me", AccessMove measures only how loud it is around you, on your iPhone. It doesn't identify sounds, and nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

AccessMove does not use the camera.

Calls and messages

When you call or text your trusted contact, a pharmacy, or 911, AccessMove opens your iPhone's Phone or Messages app. AccessMove does not send these calls or messages itself and doesn't keep a copy.

Children

AccessMove is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • You can turn off location, microphone and speech recognition for AccessMove at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security. Some features won't work without them.
  • You can change or remove your What I need and Just for now choices at any time in the app.
  • You can choose not to rate trips.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your iPhone. Anonymous community counts can't be linked back to you, so they can't be removed individually.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
