AccessMove Safety Notice

Updated November 6, 2026

AccessMove is a free, not-for-profit app that suggests accessible walking routes and guides you with voice, captions and vibration. Please read these safety notes before you use it.

Who AccessMove is for

AccessMove is designed for Deaf, hard-of-hearing, autistic, physically disabled and new-to-the-city travelers, and for anyone with a short-term need, such as walking more slowly while recovering from an injury.

AccessMove isn't designed as a navigation aid for blind or low-vision travelers. Apps made for blind travelers include Be My Eyes, Seeing AI and Lazarillo.

Stay safe on every trip

  • AccessMove is extra help. Always use your own judgment, and your usual mobility aids and support.
  • Check the route yourself. Routes come from map data that can be missing, out of date or wrong, including curbs, curb cuts, slopes, sidewalks, steps, surfaces and construction.
  • AccessMove can't see traffic or traffic lights. Always check it's safe before you cross.
  • Location (GPS) can be off by several meters, especially near tall buildings.
  • AccessMove doesn't listen for sirens, horns or other warning sounds. iPhone's Sound Recognition can alert you to some of them: Settings › Accessibility › Sound Recognition. It can miss sounds too, so stay aware of what's around you.
  • Follow traffic laws, crossing signals, and instructions from police and staff.
  • In an emergency, call 911. Don't rely on AccessMove to get help.

Not medical advice

  • AccessMove doesn't give medical, health or mental-health advice. Your choices in What I need and Just for now only change routes and guidance.
  • Calm mode is a moment of calm, not medical or mental-health care. If you're in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
  • The pharmacy, urgent care and hospital list only helps you find places. It doesn't recommend where to get care, and opening hours may be wrong, so call ahead.

Information from others

Route ratings and obstacle reports come from other people and may be wrong or out of date. Place information comes from Apple Maps and may be incomplete.
