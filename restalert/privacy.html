RestAlert Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

RestAlert helps shift workers track sleep debt and workload on iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes. The short version: your data stays on your devices and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Data the app uses

  • Sleep data from Apple Health. With your permission, RestAlert reads the sleep your Apple Watch (or other apps) record in Apple Health, and the watch app can save sleep you time with its "Going to sleep" button.
  • What you log in the app. Shifts, sleep, energy check-ins and caffeine you enter.
  • Settings. Your sleep target, work-hour rules and alert preference.

Where your data is stored

Everything is stored locally on your iPhone and Apple Watch. The two devices exchange data directly with each other using Apple's Watch Connectivity so both show the same numbers. RestAlert has no servers and no accounts, and it does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Apple Health data

Health data is used only to calculate your sleep debt and show it to you. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never stored in iCloud by RestAlert. You can change or revoke access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → RestAlert.

Notifications

Alerts about long shifts, weekly hours and recovery sleep are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification content is sent to any server.

Deleting your data

Deleting RestAlert from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes everything the app stored. Sleep the app saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Not medical advice

RestAlert is a self-tracking tool, not a medical device. Its sleep-debt and workload estimates do not diagnose any condition.

Children

RestAlert is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
