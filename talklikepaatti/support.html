Need help, or found a mistake in a Tamil word? Email srivparam17@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I record my voice?

  1. On the home screen, tap ⚙️ to open About Talk Like Paatti.
  2. Choose Family Voice and type your name, like Amma or Appa.
  3. Tap the red record button next to a word or song and say the word clearly, once. For a song, sing the whole song.
  4. Tap stop, then play to listen. Not happy with it? Tap the trash can and record again.

Can grandparents record too?

Yes. Hand them your phone when they visit (or during a video call, with the phone beside you) and let them record words in Family Voice. Their recordings stay on your device.

Paatti can't hear us

Check Settings › Talk Like Paatti › Microphone is on. If your device can't check Tamil pronunciation automatically, the app uses listen-and-compare: hear Paatti, then hear yourself.

A word looks wrong

Please email us the word. Every word is reviewed by a Tamil speaker, and we can fix or remove a word on every device within a day.
